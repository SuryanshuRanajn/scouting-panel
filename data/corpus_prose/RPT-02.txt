Scouting report RPT-02. We went to watch Brightwell and they started in a 4-4-2; flat banks of four, compact. Strengths worth respecting: compact defensive block, and also crosses from both flanks. Where we can hurt them, we noted that full-backs are poor at tracking late runs from midfield; additionally, slow to shift play when pressed. Individually, keep an eye on this: Luca Berg plays as attacking midfielder (CAM) and is dangerous because of his passing - rated 88 in our data; dictates tempo between the lines. Ivo Petrov plays as striker (ST) and is dangerous because of his aerial - rated 86 in our data; target man for crosses. From dead balls, their free kick routine stood out: short free-kick routine to the edge of the box before a driven shot.
