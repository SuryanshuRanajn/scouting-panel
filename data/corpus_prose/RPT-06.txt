Scouting report RPT-06. We went to watch Fairholt and they started in a 4-3-3; very high defensive line. Strengths worth respecting: high line compresses the pitch, and also full-backs overlap constantly. Where we can hurt them, we noted that high line is vulnerable to through balls; additionally, weak in aerial duels at the back. Individually, keep an eye on this: Jonas Weber plays as right winger (RW) and is dangerous because of his dribbling - rated 86 in our data; takes on his full-back every time. Eli Mensah plays as central midfielder (CM) and is dangerous because of his passing - rated 80 in our data; switches play quickly. From dead balls, their corner routine stood out: short corner to a player on the edge of the area.
