Scouting report RPT-07. We went to watch Glenrock and they started in a 4-1-4-1; single pivot with a flat midfield four. Strengths worth respecting: strong pressing triggers, and also good set-piece delivery. Where we can hurt them, we noted that lone holding midfielder is exposed when bypassed; additionally, left-back struggles against pace. Individually, keep an eye on this: Noah Park plays as holding midfielder (DM) and is dangerous because of his tackling - rated 85 in our data; screens the back four. Raul Ortega plays as left-back (LB) and is dangerous because of his pace - rated 70 in our data; adds width but is beaten for speed. From dead balls, their corner routine stood out: decoy runs at the near post to free a header at the penalty spot.
