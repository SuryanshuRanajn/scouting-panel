Scouting report RPT-04. We went to watch Dunmere and they started in a 4-2-3-1; double pivot shields the back four. Strengths worth respecting: possession through the double pivot, and also creative number ten. Where we can hurt them, we noted that double pivot is dragged out of position by the press; additionally, slow to reorganise after losing possession. Individually, keep an eye on this: Marco Silva plays as attacking midfielder (CAM) and is dangerous because of his dribbling - rated 89 in our data; beats players in tight spaces. Ben Clarke plays as holding midfielder (DM) and is dangerous because of his tackling - rated 82 in our data; breaks up play. From dead balls, their corner routine stood out: inswinging corners targeted at the far post.
