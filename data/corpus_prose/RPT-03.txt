Scouting report RPT-03. We went to watch Cragmoor and they started in a 3-5-2; wing-backs push up to form a front five. Strengths worth respecting: wing-backs provide width, and also strong in aerial duels. Where we can hurt them, we noted that space behind the wing-backs on transitions; additionally, goalkeeper hesitates on crosses. Individually, keep an eye on this: Dmitri Volkov plays as right wing-back (RWB) and is dangerous because of his pace - rated 85 in our data; relentless runs down the right. Karim Haddad plays as centre-back (CB) and is dangerous because of his aerial - rated 88 in our data; wins most headers. From dead balls, their throw-in routine stood out: long throw-ins into the six-yard box from the right side.
