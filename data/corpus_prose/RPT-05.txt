Individually, keep an eye on this: Ahmed Nasser plays as striker (ST) and is dangerous because of his pace - rated 87 in our data; runs the channels. Piotr Zielinski plays as centre-back (CB) and is dangerous because of his aerial - rated 83 in our data; commands the box. From dead balls, their free kick routine stood out: indirect free kicks played into a runner peeling off the wall.
