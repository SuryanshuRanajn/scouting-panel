Scouting report RPT-01. We went to watch Ashford and they started in a 4-3-3; drops into a 4-5-1 mid block without the ball. Strengths worth respecting: fast counter-attacks through the wide forwards, and also high pressing after losing the ball. Where we can hurt them, we noted that centre-backs are slow on the turn against balls in behind; additionally, right-back leaves space when the team presses high. Individually, keep an eye on this: Tomas Reyes plays as left winger (LW) and is dangerous because of his pace - rated 90 in our data; cuts inside onto his right foot. Sam Okoye plays as striker (ST) and is dangerous because of his finishing - rated 84 in our data; clinical in the box. From dead balls, their corner routine stood out: 70% of corners are whipped to the near post.
